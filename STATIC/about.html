About WOMECO

Leading the global movement for meaningful and transformative education

Who We Are

The World Meaningful Education Council (WOMECO) is an international organization dedicated to advancing quality education, fostering innovation in learning, and promoting sustainable development through education worldwide.

Founded in 2010, WOMECO emerged from the recognition that education is not merely about transmitting knowledge, but about fostering critical thinking, creativity, empathy, and the skills needed to address global challenges. We work at the intersection of policy, research, and practice to create systemic change in education systems around the world.

Our approach is rooted in collaboration. We bring together governments, educational institutions, civil society organizations, private sector partners, and communities to co-create solutions that are contextually relevant and globally informed. Through this collective action, we aim to ensure that every learner, regardless of their background or location, has access to meaningful, inclusive, and transformative education.

Our Approach

  • Evidence-based policy development and advocacy
  • Innovation in teaching and learning methodologies
  • Capacity building for educators and institutions
  • Research and knowledge sharing
  • Multi-stakeholder partnerships for systemic change
  • Focus on equity, inclusion, and sustainability

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To advance quality, equitable, and meaningful education for all by fostering innovation, building partnerships, and promoting evidence-based policies that prepare learners to thrive in an interconnected and rapidly changing world.

Our Vision

A world where every individual has access to transformative education that empowers them to realize their full potential, contribute meaningfully to society, and shape a sustainable and peaceful future for all.

Core Values

Equity

Ensuring all learners have access to quality education regardless of background

Inclusion

Creating learning environments where diversity is celebrated and all voices are heard

Innovation

Embracing new approaches and technologies to enhance learning outcomes

Collaboration

Building partnerships across sectors and borders to maximize impact

Sustainability

Promoting environmental stewardship and long-term systemic change

Excellence

Striving for the highest standards in all our work and partnerships
