World Leaders Commit $15 Billion to Global Education at Historic Summit

March 15, 2025

In a landmark moment for global education, leaders from 120 countries gathered at the WOMECO Global Education Summit in Geneva, committing unprecedented funding and policy reforms to ensure quality education for all by 2030.

The summit resulted in the "Geneva Declaration on Education," which includes binding commitments to increase education budgets, support teacher training, expand digital infrastructure, and prioritize inclusive education for marginalized communities.
