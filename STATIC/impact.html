Featured Story
Rwanda

From Zero to Digital: Rwanda's Education Transformation

In 2018, rural schools in Rwanda's Eastern Province had no internet connectivity. Today, through WOMECO's Connect to Learn initiative, 2,500 schools serve 450,000 students with high-speed internet, digital devices, and trained teachers. Literacy rates have increased by 34% and STEM enrollment has doubled.
